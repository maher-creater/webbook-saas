Overview · the pairing flow

  1. Open a verified Binance and/or RedotPay account using Maher's affiliate link below.
  2. Execute a real P2P buy of USDT (3% fee) followed by a real sell at a higher price.
  3. Open the 2030B dashboard, click Add new pairing and upload both screenshots with amounts and date.
  4. The 2030B admin (or auto-checker) verifies the screenshots — you earn +1 credit and progress your level.

Verify Binance

  1. Click Maher's Binance affiliate link — your account starts with lifetime fee discount.
  2. Sign up using your real legal name and a strong password.
  3. Open Identity → Verification. Submit ID front/back + selfie. Approval usually takes 24h.
  4. Enable 2FA (Google Authenticator + SMS).
  5. Deposit a small fiat amount via your local payment method, or buy USDT via the P2P tab.
  6. You're ready to perform real P2P trades on Binance — required for Level 3.

How to make a pairing

Buy leg

  1. Open the P2P tab on your platform.
  2. Choose a seller with a good rating offering USDT in your local currency.
  3. Place a buy order — expect ~3% fee.
  4. Send the fiat via the seller's payment method.
  5. Confirm release of USDT to your wallet.
  6. Take a clean screenshot showing amount, fiat total, fees, and timestamp.

Sell leg

  1. Back on the P2P tab, choose a buyer paying a higher fiat rate.
  2. Post a sell ad or take an existing buy order.
  3. Send USDT to escrow; the buyer transfers fiat to your account.
  4. Confirm fiat received and release USDT.
  5. Take a clean screenshot of the completed sell.
  6. Result: ≈10% profit on capital → +1 credit on 2030B.

Screenshot rules

A valid screenshot lets the 2030B verifier approve your pairing in seconds. Make sure each capture contains:

  • USDT amount and fiat amount
  • Transaction date & time
  • Transaction ID / reference number
  • Platform name visible (Binance or RedotPay)
  • Status = Completed
  • No personal data of counterparty (mask it)

Format: PNG, JPG, or WebP · Max 5 MB · No editing/photoshopping.
