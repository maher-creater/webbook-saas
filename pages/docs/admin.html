Sub-admin

Sub-Admin manual

You are a verifier. You approve or reject pending pairings and ops. You can issue penalties for fake submissions. You cannot create other admins or change global config.

Overview

Your job is to keep the platform honest. Every pairing has 2 screenshots (buy + sell). You compare amounts, dates, UIDs, and decide approve / reject.

  • Tabs you see depend on the permissions your super-admin granted you.
  • Every approve / reject writes to audit_log with your admin id.
  • Wrongful approvals can themselves be penalized by the super-admin.

Sign-in

Go to /pages/admin.php. Enter the email + password your super-admin issued. After 5 failed attempts in 1 minute, your IP is rate-limited.

Verifying a pairing

  1. Open the Pending tab.
  2. Click each screenshot to view it full-size.
  3. Check: amounts match, date is recent, exchange UID matches the user's profile, no obvious photo manipulation.
  4. Click Approve (or Reject with a reason). The user is notified.

Single ops vs pairings

A pairing is a buy + sell of equal USDT amount (worth 1 credit when verified). A single op is a one-sided trade (counts toward level progression but does not by itself produce a credit). Both go through the same approval flow.

Common rejection reasons

  • Screenshot is cropped — missing date, amount, or UID.
  • Different exchange UID from the one in the user's profile.
  • Buy and sell amounts differ by more than 0.5 USDT.
  • Date is more than 7 days old.
  • Screenshot is reused (system flags duplicate perceptual hash).

Issuing a penalty

If a user submitted a forged screenshot, open Penalties → New penalty. Pick rule user_fake_pairing. The credits delta is auto-filled from penalties.json. Add evidence (the pairing id).

Tip: always reject the pairing first, then issue the penalty. The user receives both notifications.

Users tab

List of every user with their level, credits, pairings, referral, joined-at. You can toggle a user active/inactive — they keep their data but cannot sign in or submit until reactivated.

Etiquette

  • Always leave a rejection reason — users can appeal.
  • Never disable a user without an audit-loggable reason.
  • If you find a fraud ring, escalate to the super-admin before issuing bulk penalties.
  • Your own activity is audited too. Be fair and consistent.
