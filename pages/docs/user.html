2030B P2P Pairing — User guide

Earn credits by doing the simple thing you already do: buy USDT, sell USDT. Each verified buy + sell pair gives you 1 credit. Credits unlock levels and can be redeemed into our ecosystem currencies.

Getting started in 3 steps

  1. Sign up at /auth with your email + password.
  2. Complete your profile (country, exchange UID, phone).
  3. Submit your first pairing from your dashboard.

Sign up & verify

Password must be at least security.json > auth.password_min_length characters. After signup you get an 8-character verification code — paste it into the verify panel.

If you arrived via someone's affiliate link (?ref=CODE), they automatically become your referrer at signup — nothing for you to do.

Choose a P2P provider

The platform supports multiple exchanges (Binance, RedotPay, and any others the super-admin enables). Each provider has its own min USDT, fee, supported fiat, and level requirement. The dashboard wizard shows only the providers you can use at your current level.

Submit a pairing

  1. Click Add pairing on the dashboard.
  2. Step 1: pick the provider.
  3. Step 2: enter your buy details (USDT, fiat amount, fiat currency, date) and upload the buy screenshot.
  4. Step 3: enter your sell details and screenshot.
  5. Step 4: review. Submit. Status is pending until a sub-admin verifies.

Screenshot rules

  • Show UID, amount, date, status — no crops.
  • PNG, JPG, or WEBP only. Max 5 MB.
  • No edits, no annotations. EXIF is stripped on upload.
  • Re-using a screenshot triggers automatic perceptual-hash detection → instant penalty.

Redeem ecosystem currencies

2030B credits are keys — they unlock currencies in 10 sister projects (CTC for cybersecurity, GTC for gaming, HTC for health, …). Each project has its own rate. Browse them on the Solutions page.

Affiliate

You earn extra credits when friends sign up with your link and verify pairings. See the Affiliate program docs.

Privacy & GDPR

You can export or delete your data anytime from Profile → Privacy. After deletion, your account is anonymized (referral history kept for ledger integrity). Retention is configured in security.json > data_privacy.

FAQ

How long does verification take?

Usually under 24 hours. You'll see the status flip in your dashboard.

My pairing was rejected — can I appeal?

Yes — open the rejected card, click Appeal, and add evidence. A different admin reviews appeals.

Do I need to sell to the same person I bought from?

No. Both legs just need to be real P2P transactions on the same provider.

What happens to my credits if I lose my account?

Sign up with the same email and contact a super-admin — the audit log lets us restore your balance.
