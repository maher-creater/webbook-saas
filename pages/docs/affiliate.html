Affiliate program

Share 2030B. Every time someone signs up with your link and earns a credit, you get a kickback — automatically, forever.

How it works

  1. You sign up — the system assigns you a unique ref_code.
  2. You share https://2030b.com/?ref=YOUR_CODE with friends.
  3. A friend signs up with that link — they become your referee permanently.
  4. Every time your referee verifies a pairing, you get a percentage of their credit (default 10%, max 5 / pairing).
  5. Your kickback lands directly in your total_credits.

Your code & link

Open Affiliate in the main menu. You'll see your code, your share link, copy buttons, and pre-filled WhatsApp / X / Telegram / email share buttons.

Kickback rules

Controlled by super-admin in config.json > affiliate_program:

{
  "affiliate_program": {
    "enabled": true,
    "percent_of_pairing_credits": 10,
    "max_per_pairing": 5
  }
}

Example: a referee verifies a pairing worth 50 credits → you get min(50 × 10%, 5) = 5 credits.

Track your referrals

The affiliate page shows three KPIs: total referrals, rewards paid, and credits kicked-back. The table lists each referee with their join date and how much they've earned you so far.

Payout in credits

Your affiliate credits are real credits — you can redeem them into any ecosystem currency (CTC, GTC, …) at the current rate, or use them to unlock perks.

Anti-abuse rules

  • Self-referrals are blocked (same IP / same email).
  • If your referee is found to have submitted fake pairings, their credits are clawed back — and your kickback with them.
  • Mass-registered fake referees → permanent affiliate ban (penalty: affiliate_abuse).
  • You can only have one referrer (the first link that brought you in).

FAQ

How long does the kickback last?

Forever, as long as your referee keeps earning credits.

Can I share my code on social media?

Yes — use the pre-filled share buttons on the affiliate page.
